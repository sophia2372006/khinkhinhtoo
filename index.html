Biography

Khin Khin Htoo (ခင်ခင်ထူး) was born in December 1965 in Myingyan, Myanmar. She is a Myanmar novelist and short-story writer whose works are widely recognised for their portrayal of traditional Burmese culture and the lives of people in Upper Myanmar. She entered the literary world in 1993 with her short story Pann Pan Ba Ya Ze (ပန်းပန်ပါရစေ), marking the beginning of a writing career that has produced more than one hundred short stories and numerous other literary works. Among her best-known publications are Ma Eain Kan (မအိမ်ကံ), Pan Kyar Wut Hmone (ပန်းကြာဝတ်မှုန်), and Taw Minthamee (တောမင်းသမီး). Her short-story collection Phat Sein Kyun Taung Shwe Wuttu-to Mya (ဖက်စိမ်းကွမ်းတောင်ရွှေ ဝတ္ထုတိုများ) received the Myanmar National Literature Award in 2003. Several of her stories have also been adapted into films, allowing her writing to reach audiences beyond book readers.

Style & Legacy

Khin Khin Htoo's writing is known for its straightforward storytelling, memorable characters, and detailed descriptions of traditional Myanmar life. Her stories frequently explore family relationships, village communities, local customs, and the experiences of ordinary people. By including regional expressions and cultural practices in her narratives, she gives readers a closer understanding of life in Upper Myanmar.
